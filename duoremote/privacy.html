Duo Remote IQ privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Duo Remote IQ collects no personal data. It is a screen-sharing client that connects your iPhone or iPad directly to your own Mac. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we receive no information about you or how you use it.

What stays on your device

  • The computers you save (name, address, port and username) are stored only on your device, along with each Mac's identity (its SSH host key fingerprint) once you trust it.
  • Passwords you choose to save are kept in your device's Keychain and are only ever used to sign in to the computer you are connecting to.
  • The connection goes straight from your device to your computer. Nothing passes through our servers; we don't run any.
  • Photos and files you choose to send to your Mac go straight to its Downloads folder over the encrypted connection. The app only reads the items you pick.

Connection security

With Encrypt on for a Mac (the default for new Macs), the whole session (screen, typing, clipboard and files) runs inside SSH to the Mac's built-in Remote Login, and the app checks the Mac's identity before sending your password. With Encrypt off, your password isn't sent in the clear, but the rest of the session is unencrypted and the app can't confirm it is talking to your computer; use that only on a network you trust.

Local network access

iOS asks for permission to use your local network so the app can reach computers on it. That permission is used only to find Macs offering Screen Sharing on your network and to connect to the computers you choose.

Clipboard

If you use the clipboard features, text is copied between your iPhone and the computer you are connected to. We never receive it. If Universal Clipboard is on, Apple's system may also pass it to your other devices.

Children

Duo Remote IQ collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's privacy details on the App Store before the new version ships.
