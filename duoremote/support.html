Setting up your Mac

  1. On the Mac, open System Settings → General → Sharing and turn on Screen Sharing.
  2. Click the ⓘ next to Screen Sharing and make sure your user is allowed.
  3. For an encrypted connection (recommended), also turn on Remote Login in the same list.
  4. In Duo Remote IQ, your Mac usually appears under On this network: tap it. Or add it by the address shown in Sharing (for example 192.168.1.20 or Your-Mac.local), port 5900. Enter your Mac username and password.
  5. The first time you connect encrypted, the app shows your Mac's identity (a fingerprint). To check it, run ssh-keygen -lf /etc/ssh/ssh_host_ed25519_key.pub in Terminal on the Mac, then tap Trust and Connect.

Common questions

Does it need an iPhone Duo? No. It's designed for iPhone Duo: open it in laptop pose and the Mac's screen sits on top with a full-size keyboard and trackpad below. It also works folded, with a keyboard you can show or hide. On any other iPhone running iOS 26 or later, the Mac's screen sits above a smaller keyboard and trackpad. On iPad, the Mac fills the screen, and the keyboard and trackpad slide up when you need them; a hardware keyboard, mouse or trackpad works too.

Can I see the Mac full screen, or use my phone as just a keyboard and trackpad? Yes. In a session, open the menu (•••) and choose View: Mac and Keyboard, Mac Only, or Keyboard & Trackpad Only. Turn an iPhone sideways and the Mac fills the screen on its own. In Mac Only you tap where you want to click; turn on Trackpad on Screen in the menu to drag anywhere and move the pointer instead, like a mouse.

Can I lock my saved passwords? Yes. On the first screen, turn on “Lock Saved Passwords”. The app then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before using a saved password.

Can it connect over the internet? Use a VPN to your home network and connect with Encrypt on. Don't expose your Mac's Screen Sharing or Remote Login ports directly to the internet.

Is the connection encrypted? Yes, when Encrypt is on for that Mac (the default for new Macs). The session then runs inside SSH to your Mac's built-in Remote Login, so the screen, your typing and the clipboard are all encrypted, and the app checks it's really your Mac. With Encrypt off (for a Mac without Remote Login), only the sign-in is protected; use that only on a home network you trust.

How do I send a photo or file to my Mac? In an encrypted session, open the menu and choose Send to Mac’s Downloads. Files up to 200 MB go straight to the Downloads folder. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → General → Sharing, tap ⓘ next to Remote Login and turn on “Allow full disk access for remote users”.

Does it work with Windows? Not yet. It's built and tested for Macs using macOS Screen Sharing. Other VNC servers may work but aren't supported yet.

It's slow. Try "Reduced colour" for that computer, or set the Mac's display to a lower resolution in System Settings → Displays. A 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection helps.

Why did my Mac's clipboard go empty? With clipboard sharing on, copying emoji or non-Western text on your iPhone can clear the Mac's clipboard. Clipboard sharing handles plain Western text only and is off by default.

Is there a subscription? No. There are no in-app payments.
