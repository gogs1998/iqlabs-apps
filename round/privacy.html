Round privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 (revised)

Round collects no personal data. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no internet connection. We receive no information about you or how you use it.

Apple Watch and heart rate

If you use Round on Apple Watch, the Watch app reads your heart rate and active energy during a session (through Apple Health, with your permission) to show them between rounds and to draw the session graph. It sends that summary only to Round on your own paired iPhone, where it is kept in a file on the device. Sessions are saved as workouts in Health and Fitness on your Watch. Round itself sends nothing anywhere: no account, no server, no analytics. Your History is included in your normal iPhone backup, and workouts sync through Apple Health as you have set it up. You can delete any session in History.

iPhone

The iPhone app does not ask to read your health data. Your timers, settings and history stay on your iPhone.

Children

Round collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's privacy details on the App Store before the new version ships.
