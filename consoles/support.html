Relics: Consoles support

Help with Relics: Consoles for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad

Get in touch

Email gordon@iqlabs.app with your device, iOS version, the system you were playing and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I add my games? Put the game files you own in the Files app, then add them from the shelf. They are copied into the app's own folder, which you can also see in Files. A .zip holding one game works too.

Does it come with games? Only a few free homebrew games, shared by their authors under free licences. It includes no other games and no console system software, and it never downloads any. Use it only with games you own.

Does it need an iPhone Duo? No. It works on any iPhone or iPad. On iPhone Duo, open it like a laptop and the game stands on the top screen while a full-size controller lies flat under your thumbs. Close it to pause and save.

Can I use a game controller? Yes. Bluetooth controllers and keyboards work, with remapping and a second player.

Where are my saves? On your device, in the app's folder in Files. Back that folder up if you want to keep them.

Is there a subscription? No. It is a one-off purchase with no in-app payments.

Where is the source code? Relics: Consoles is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. The source is at github.com/gogs1998/relics-consoles.

Privacy: the app collects no data. See the privacy policy.
