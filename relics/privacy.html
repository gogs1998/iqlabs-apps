Relics privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Relics collects no personal data. Relics is a museum of tiny computers you can use. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we receive no information about you or how you use it.

What stays on your device

  • Relics does not connect to the internet. Its web browser shows an invented, offline 1999 web built into the app.
  • BASIC programs, documents, drawings and high scores you make are saved only on your device.

Children

Relics collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's privacy details on the App Store before the new version ships.
