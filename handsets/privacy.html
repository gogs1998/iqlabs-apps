Relics: Phones privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Relics: Phones collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, makes no internet connections, and we receive no information about you or how you use it.

Contacts

If you allow it, the app reads the names and phone numbers in your contacts to show them in its phonebooks. It never changes them or sends them anywhere. Everything still works if you say no.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while you are taking a photo inside the app. Photos are kept in the app's own gallery and, if you allow it, added to your photo library with add-only access, so the app cannot see your other photos.

Texts and calls

Texts you write are sent through your iPhone's own message sheet, and calls go through its own call prompt; you confirm both. The app does not receive what you send.

What stays on your device

  • Sent lists, call logs, drafts, learned words, scores, settings and story progress are stored only on your device.
  • The people in the in-app stories and on the phones' previous-owner data are invented and live inside the app. Nothing is sent or received.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app's privacy details on the App Store before the new version ships.
