Help with Relics: Phones for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad

Get in touch

Email gordon@iqlabs.app with your device, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Does it need an iPhone Duo? No. It works on any iPhone or iPad. On iPhone Duo, open it like a laptop and the phone's screen stands up while its keys lie flat under your thumbs.

Do the texts and calls really go out? Yes, through your iPhone's own message sheet and call prompt, and you confirm each one. An iPad or an iPhone without a SIM can't send texts or make calls.

Who are the people already on the phones? They are invented. In Settings, "Whose phones?" lets you follow one life across every phone, give each phone a different previous owner, or start with just your own stuff.

Is there a subscription? No. It is a one-off purchase with no in-app payments.

Privacy: the app collects no data. See the privacy policy.
